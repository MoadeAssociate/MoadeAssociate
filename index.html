ABOUT US

ABOUT US

Law that Moves with Vision

MOADE Associates is a forward-thinking virtual legal practice and consulting firm dedicated to empowering businesses and individuals to thrive in Nigeria’s dynamic landscape. With a deep understanding of the local market and a proven track record of delivering exceptional results, we offer a comprehensive suite of legal services tailored to meet our clients’ unique needs.

Moade Associates was born from a simple belief, that the law should not only protect ideas, but propel them. We exist for the bold; for entrepreneurs, creators, and leaders who are building tomorrow’s Africa today. Our role is to make sure your vision stands on solid ground, legally sound, strategically aligned, and ready for growth.

We are a collective of legal and strategic minds working at the intersection of commerce, creativity, and purpose. Our clients are diverse, from founders and family offices to creative entrepreneurs, growing enterprises, and corporate organizations. But what connects them all is a drive to build with integrity, clarity, and impact. That’s the kind of work we love, work that changes how business is done and how stories are told.

At the heart of our practice are our core values: integrity, excellence, and a steadfast commitment to client success. We leverage technology to deliver efficient and accessible legal services, ensuring our clients receive the support they need, when they need it.

Whether you are a budding entrepreneur, a well-established enterprise, or an individual seeking legal counsel, MOADE Associates is your trusted partner in achieving your goals.
